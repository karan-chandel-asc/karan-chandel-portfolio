Legal Tech

Lexora — Legal AI Case Management

A workspace for law firms to manage cases, find answers in their own documents, and keep hearings in one place.

Details

Problems it solves

  • A lawyer needs a clause from a 200-page PDF and cannot remember which file it was in
  • Associates spend hours re-reading the same case files the night before a hearing
  • Generic AI answers cannot be used with a client because there is no source to point to
  • Hearing dates live in notebooks or WhatsApp chats and get missed
  • Cases, documents, and notes are scattered across folders, email, and spreadsheets
  • A new associate joining a matter has no fast way to ask the file “what happened so far?”

Technical highlights

  • PDF upload pipeline: Django saves the file, Celery chunks and embeds, Pinecone stores vectors by case
  • Case-aware RAG chat on FastAPI + LangGraph + Groq, with answers tied back to source passages
  • Docker Compose stack deployed on AWS EC2
Django FastAPI Pinecone Groq

In development testing: 40+ uploaded case PDFs across 3 practice areas — response time under 4s per query. Built and deployed solo — from schema design to AWS deployment — over several weeks.

Customer Support

SupportAI — Customer Support Automation

Inbound emails become classified, prioritized, and assigned tickets — with a separate queue for admins and agents.

Details

Problems it solves

  • Support emails sit unread for hours because nobody triaged the shared inbox
  • An angry VIP looks the same as a password-reset in a pile of unsorted mail
  • Agents cherry-pick easy tickets and SLA on the hard ones slips
  • Managers cannot see volume, sentiment, or who owns what without a spreadsheet
  • The same “how do I reset…” reply gets typed out by hand every day
  • Weekend mail piles up until Monday with no routing and no owner

Technical highlights

  • IMAP poll of a shared support inbox creates or matches a customer and opens a ticket
  • Groq classifies category, priority, and sentiment, with a rule-based fallback
  • Admins see the full workspace; agents only see tickets in My Queue
Django IMAP Groq

Document AI

Fieldline — Document Intelligence

Upload PDFs, extract the fields, check them on the page, then export JSON or CSV — without retyping invoices.

Details

Problems it solves

  • Someone retypes every invoice into a spreadsheet by hand
  • A wrong amount gets copied and nobody notices until the client calls
  • Different document layouts break a one-size-fits-all template
  • Ops cannot tell which files failed versus which still need a human look
  • Extracted data has no way back to the PDF page it came from
  • Shipping JSON to another system means another round of copy-paste

Technical highlights

  • Celery renders each page with PyMuPDF and captures word boxes
  • Groq returns structured JSON, then values are matched onto the page
  • Human review UI with a live page image and highlighted matches
PyMuPDF Celery Groq

In development testing: sample invoices, receipts, and freight bills — extract, review on the page, then export.

Hospitality

Deskline — AI Voice Agents for Hospitality

AI answers the hotel or restaurant phone, books a room or a table, and logs the call — no Retell or Vapi.

Details

Problems it solves

  • Front desk misses calls when the lobby is busy
  • Guests hang up instead of waiting on hold to book a room or a table
  • After-hours bookings go to voicemail and never get followed up
  • Staff retype the same reservation details into the system after every call
  • There is no record of what the caller asked or whether a booking was made
  • Hotels do not want another monthly voice-AI wrapper fee on top of the phone bill

Technical highlights

  • Owned stack: Twilio ConversationRelay + Groq + Django + FastAPI
  • Tool calling for book_room and book_table, saved to the local database
  • Dashboard, call history, and analytics for outcome and sentiment
Twilio Groq FastAPI
